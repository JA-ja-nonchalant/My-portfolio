About Me

Brief Bio UI/UX & Front‑End Focus

I am a Bachelor of Science in Information Technology (BSIT) graduate from City College of Calamba and a certified NC III passer. My focus is on UI/UX design and front-end development, where I strive to create meaningful, intuitive, and visually balanced digital experiences. I draw inspiration from real-life interactions, design culture, and continuous learning. My goal is to craft interfaces that not only look visually appealing but also enhance how users connect with information.
